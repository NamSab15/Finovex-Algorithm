General

What is Finovex?

Finovex Algorithm Pvt. Ltd. builds the operating system for money. HEDG runs finance for growing businesses, SORT.it sorts personal money every day, and our services desk handles registrations, compliance and specialist advisory.

Where are you based, and who do you work with?

We are based in Jaipur, Rajasthan, and work with founders and businesses across India, in metros and Tier 2 cities alike.

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?

Every enquiry gets a response within 24 to 48 hours. Use the contact form, or write to company@finovexalgorithm.com.

Which email should I use for what?

HEDG enquiries go to finance@hedg.business, SORT.it to company@we-sort-it.in, and everything else to company@finovexalgorithm.com. The contact form routes your message automatically.

HEDG

What does HEDG actually do?

HEDG is the finance office for growing businesses: bookkeeping and reconciliations, GST, TDS, ITR and ROC filings, monthly MIS, cash forecasting and Virtual CFO support, delivered as one engagement.

Do I need to install new software?

No. HEDG is delivered by our team on email, WhatsApp and Excel from day one, and moves onto the HEDG platform progressively as modules go live.

What does onboarding include?

A kickoff call, document setup and 30 days of catch up bookkeeping to bring your books current before the regular cadence begins.

Is HEDG available now?

Early access is open and onboarding a limited number of companies. Join the HEDG waitlist and we will email you when your invite is ready.

HEDG pricing & billing

How much does HEDG cost?

Essential is ₹22,500 a month and Growth is ₹45,000 a month, both exclusive of GST. Custom plans cover groups, multi entity structures and special situations. Annual billing gives you 2 months free.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, any month. As your transaction volumes grow we right size the plan and prorate the difference. No lock in beyond the current month.

Is there a pilot?

Growth starts with a 30 day pilot. If it is not working, you walk away with your books, filings and data, clean and complete.

Are taxes included in the price?

All prices are exclusive of GST. Invoices are GST compliant with input credit available where applicable.

How do cancellations work?

30 days written notice. You receive your books, filings and data in full. Payment obligations accrued before termination still apply.

SORT.it

When does SORT.it launch?

SORT.it is launching soon. Join the waitlist and we will send you one email the moment it is ready.

What will SORT.it do?

It brings your accounts, expenses, savings, investments and liabilities into one view, categorises transactions automatically, and turns them into budgets, alerts, a net worth dashboard and a financial health score.

Is SORT.it the same as HEDG?

No. HEDG is for businesses and their finance operations. SORT.it is for individuals and their personal money.

Services

Which services do you offer?

Loans and credit, advisory and Virtual CFO in house; registration and compliance (startups, licenses, trademarks, GST, income tax, MCA); and Company Secretary, legal, investment banking, valuation, investment advisory and insurance through our vetted partner network.

How is pricing decided for services?

Every business is sized differently, so services are quoted for your specific scope. Send an enquiry and we will come back with a quote.

Are partner services included in HEDG plans?

Partner network services are delivered by licensed external professionals and are quoted separately. We introduce, coordinate and stay accountable for the relationship.

Data, privacy & emails

Do you sell my data?

No. Finovex does not sell personal or business financial information to third parties. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

What cookies do you use?

Essential cookies keep the site working. With your consent, we also set a first party cookie that remembers your visit, so we can understand which products interest you and tailor the updates we send. You can choose essential only from the cookie banner.

How do I stop receiving emails?

Every email we send has a one click unsubscribe link. You can also write to company@finovexalgorithm.com and we will remove you.
